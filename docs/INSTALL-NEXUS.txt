Squad Status HUD 1.1.0 — Installation
16 November 2026 (16/11/2026)

Close the game before you start.

1. Find your game folder in Steam: right-click the game, then choose
   Manage > Browse local files. Open StarshipTroopers_UBW > Binaries > Win64.
2. Download UE4SS_v4.0.0-6-gef15eba8.zip and extract it:
   https://github.com/UE4SS-RE/RE-UE4SS/releases/download/experimental-latest/UE4SS_v4.0.0-6-gef15eba8.zip
3. Copy dwmapi.dll and the ue4ss folder into Win64. Check that dwmapi.dll
   is beside StarshipTroopers_UBW-Win64-Shipping.exe, with UE4SS.dll inside
   Win64/ue4ss. Skip this step if this UE4SS build is already installed.
4. Download Squad Status HUD from Nexus Mods and extract it:
   https://www.nexusmods.com/starshiptroopersultimatebugwar/mods/15
5. Copy the mod's ue4ss folder into the same Win64 folder. Merge the folders.
   Check that Win64/ue4ss/Mods/SquadStatusHUD/Scripts/main.lua exists.
6. Start the game as usual, load a mission and recruit some troopers.
   Their names and health bars will appear above the minimap.

For example, your Win64 folder might be:
N:\SteamLibrary\steamapps\common\StarshipTroopers_UltimateBugWar\StarshipTroopers_UBW\Binaries\Win64
Your drive and install location may be different.

Requires Windows 10/11, Steam build 22832378 and UE4SS. Tested at 1920x1080.
For help with UE4SS: https://docs.ue4ss.com/installation-guide.html

To move or resize the roster, change Left, Bottom or Scale in
ue4ss/Mods/SquadStatusHUD/Scripts/config.lua, then restart the game.

To uninstall, close the game and delete ue4ss/Mods/SquadStatusHUD.
Keep UE4SS if you use it for other mods.
Made with AI by NEXara. See CREDITS.txt.

Squad Alerts is included. New members fade in; confirmed deaths get a brief red emphasis.
Health bars turn red at 30% or less and pulse slowly at 15% or less.
Low health does not display a text notification.
